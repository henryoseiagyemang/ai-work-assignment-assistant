The Problem

Turning requirements into work is broken

Three pain points every team faces when going from document to delivery

Manual Decomposition

Reading a requirements doc and manually breaking it into actionable work items is slow, inconsistent, and error-prone.

No Traceability

Once work is assigned, the link back to the original requirement is lost. You can't prove every requirement was covered.

Opaque Assignment

Work gets assigned by gut feel or availability. There's no transparent reasoning behind who got what and why.

The Cost

The gap between request and delivery

Requirements disappear into a black box. What comes out the other side is untraceable.

Transparent Matching

Not a black box — visible rules

The matching engine uses four deterministic rules, and the reasoning is always shown

1
Filter by skill overlap — only people who have at least one required skill are considered.
2
Exclude unavailable — anyone marked unavailable is removed from the candidate pool.
3
Prefer level meets difficulty — people whose skill level is at or above the work item's difficulty are preferred.
4
Prefer fully available — among equal candidates, fully available people are chosen over partially available.
Every assignment shows its reasoning in plain English
